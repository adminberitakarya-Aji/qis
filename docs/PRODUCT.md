# Qis

Version: 1.0

---

# Overview

Qis is an AI-Assisted Grid Trading Platform designed to help traders build intelligent grid strategies using Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike traditional trading bots, Qis does not allow AI to trade autonomously.

AI acts as a Strategy Planner, while the trader remains in full control of every trading decision.

The platform focuses on Spot Trading with intelligent strategy generation, adaptive grid planning, and transparent AI reasoning.

---

# Vision

Build the smartest AI-assisted Grid Trading Platform that empowers traders to make better decisions without giving full control to AI.

---

# Mission

Provide a simple, transparent, and intelligent trading platform where AI helps traders build profitable grid strategies based on market conditions.

# Core Principles

The platform is built on five principles.

## AI Assists

AI provides recommendations.

AI never replaces the trader.

---

## User Control

Users always decide:

- Capital
- Exchange
- Trading Pair
- Number of Sections
- Capital Allocation

AI cannot modify user decisions.

---

## Transparency

Every AI recommendation must include an explanation.

No black-box decisions.

---

## Deterministic Execution

AI creates strategies.

The system executes strategies.

Execution never depends on AI after approval.

---

## Simplicity

The platform should remain simple enough for beginner traders while providing powerful tools for experienced traders.

# Trading Philosophy

Qis is based on Adaptive Multi-Section Grid Trading.

Unlike traditional grid trading, Qis divides capital into multiple Sections.

Each Section may have:

- Different number of Grids
- Different Grid Distance
- Different Section Gap

Grid Distance remains constant inside a Section.

Section Gap is calculated from the last Grid of the previous Section to the first Grid of the next Section.

This allows the strategy to better handle significant market declines while preserving capital allocation.
